Why these filters?

Evidence before action

Support controls how often products must occur together; confidence controls how often B follows A. Lift above 1 means B is more common with A than in an average basket.

↑Rules meeting the selected thresholds with lift > 1 are retained. Rejected candidates remain available for comparison.

Method note

Association is a clue, not a cause.

Each invoice is one transaction. Products are identified by StockCode and labelled with Description. A high lift points to an unusual co-occurrence; it does not prove a bundle or promotion will increase sales.
